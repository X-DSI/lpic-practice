- `lpic serve`: take the 10 mock exams in a browser (FastAPI + Jinja2 + htmx, on 127.0.0.1, works offline). One question per page with a 1–60 navigator, marks for review, a server-enforced 90-minute deadline with automatic submit, and answers saved as you go so a refresh or restart resumes the mock. No keys or explanations are shown until you submit.
- Mock results: the estimated 200–800 score with the raw percentage and likely pass at 500, breakdowns by topic and objective, time used, and weak areas below the pass point.
- Review after a mock: every question with your answer, the key, rationales and the explanation; **Looks right** records a sign-off and **Flag** records a flag. `lpic reviews export` prints the sign-offs as YAML for the planner.
- Results database schema version 2 (proposed results-database-v2 ADR): `sessions.mock_id`, `drafts` and `reviews`, with a v1 database migrated in place on open. Mock answers count towards the weak-area accuracy that `lpic practice` uses.
